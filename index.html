03 / CONTROL BY DESIGN

Your provider.
Your credentials.
Your choice.

ImagineOutfit is a Bring Your Own Key app. It does not operate an image-generation backend.

◇

Direct generation

Photos and prompts go to the compatible image API provider and endpoint you configure.

⌁

Credentials stay local

API credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain, not in an ImagineOutfit account.

○

No ads or tracking

No ImagineOutfit account, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking is used.

↺

Local controls

Clear credentials, delete saved looks, and reset local app data from Settings.

ACCESS

Try the complete experience for seven days.

The trial is a free, non-consumable App Store item. It never renews and never charges automatically. After it ends, an optional one-time purchase unlocks lifetime app access.

Good to know

The trial and lifetime purchase unlock the app only. Image-generation credits are not included. Your chosen provider may charge separately for API usage.
