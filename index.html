Wear what you imagine.Your photo. Your idea. Your image API.

Your photo stays the reference. Your idea leads the look.

ImagineOutfit is an AI outfit styling app for iPhone and iPad. It visualizes a preset, a written idea, or an inspiration image on your own photo. It does not inventory a closet or mix clothes you already own.

Choose a whole outfit, top, or bottom. Review the face, pose, proportions, hands, background, and clothing details before you save or share. Results are inspiration, not a fit or shopping guarantee.

From the first photo to a saved look.

Your provider. Your credentials. Your choice.

ImagineOutfit is a Bring Your Own Key app. It does not operate an image-generation backend and does not create an ImagineOutfit account.

Direct generation

Photos and prompts go to the compatible image API provider and endpoint you configure.

Credentials stay local

API keys and Tencent Cloud credential pairs are stored in the iOS Keychain, not in an ImagineOutfit account.

No ads or tracking

The app includes no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash SDK, or cross-app tracking.

Local controls

Clear credentials, delete saved looks, and reset local app data from Settings.

Download free. Generate two previews.

The app is free on the App Store. After you connect a compatible image API, you can save two generated-image previews. Starting with the third saved image, an optional one-time purchase unlocks lifetime generation. There is no subscription.

Credits are separate

Free previews and Lifetime Access cover app permission only. Image-generation credits are not included. Your chosen provider may charge for every API request, including the first two. Lifetime Access is listed on the App Store as Lifetime Access.
