ImagineOutfit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

ImagineOutfit creates AI-generated outfit inspiration from photos and descriptions selected by the user. The app offers a seven-day free trial and an optional one-time lifetime unlock through Apple's In-App Purchase system. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

Data Processed

  • Reference photos and optional inspiration images selected by the user.
  • Outfit descriptions, prompts, style preferences, notes, and feedback.
  • Generated images and saved looks.
  • The image API provider, endpoint, model, and API credentials entered by the user, including Tencent Cloud SecretId and SecretKey.
  • Verified App Store transaction status and purchase dates for the Trial and Lifetime Access products.

Use and Storage

Selected images and prompts are sent to the image API endpoint configured by the user. Generated looks and preferences are stored locally. API keys and Tencent Cloud credential pairs are stored in the iOS Keychain. Tencent SecretKey is used locally to sign requests.

Each saved look retains a local reference-photo copy for comparison. Face, body, hand landmarks and segmentation are processed on the device; they are not stored as a biometric profile or sent to another analysis service.

Image files, protected collection backups and temporary recovery files remain on this device and are excluded from backup. Generation networking does not persist response caches, cookies or URL credentials. Generated images may be downloaded from public HTTPS locations returned by the configured provider after ImagineOutfit validates the response.

The selected provider may associate requests with the provider account represented by the credentials, and may process, retain, log, or bill API requests under its own terms and privacy policy.

StoreKit verifies trial and lifetime transactions on the device. ImagineOutfit does not send purchase history to an ImagineOutfit account or backend. Apple's purchase processing and records are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

User Controls

  • Clear the saved API key from Settings.
  • Delete saved looks and local generated files.
  • Reset onboarding, API settings, language, style preferences, reference-photo copies, collection backups, temporary recovery files and local network caches. Reset invalidates in-flight local work and prevents older results from being saved. It does not revoke data already sent to a provider or cancel provider charges.
  • Export images to Photos only when choosing to save them.

Tracking and Advertising

ImagineOutfit does not use user content for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking and does not sell personal information.

Important Limits

AI outputs are styling inspiration only. They are not fit, body-shape, health, or purchase guarantees.
