About ImagineOutfit

ImagineOutfit, branded 灵感穿搭 in Chinese, is an iOS and iPadOS app that turns a user’s photo and a clothing idea into personalized AI outfit previews. It is live on the Apple App Store. Zhichao Wei develops and sells it.

Facts reviewed: 16 September 2026

One sentence
ImagineOutfit lets you see an imagined outfit on your own photo by sending that photo and prompt to an image API account you already have.
Official names
English name: ImagineOutfit. Chinese name: 灵感穿搭. English line: Wear what you imagine. Chinese line: 把灵感穿上身. App Store English subtitle: AI Outfit Generator & Stylist. App Store Chinese subtitle: AI换装与虚拟试衣造型助手.
Who made it
Zhichao Wei develops, operates, and sells ImagineOutfit. The App Store seller name is 智超 卫. There is no separate company name in the public listing. Contact: weizhichao1027@gmail.com, +86 135 0176 2709.
Platforms
ImagineOutfit is available for iPhone and iPad and requires iOS 17.0 or later, or iPadOS 17.0 or later. It is not available on Google Play or Android.
Store listing
The app is live on the App Store. Apple ID: 6782911564. Bundle ID: com.weizhichao30.stylemuse. Version observed on 16 September 2026: 1.1.0. Category: Lifestyle. Age rating: 4+. Official link: apps.apple.com/app/id6782911564.
Pricing
The app downloads for free. After you connect a compatible image API, you can save two generated-image previews. Generation from the third saved image requires the optional one-time non-consumable Lifetime Access. There is no subscription. On 16 September 2026 the App Store listed Lifetime Access at USD 4.99 in the United States and CNY 38.00 in mainland China. Apple storefront prices can change. Provider API usage is billed by the provider and is not included.
How generation works
ImagineOutfit does not run an image-generation server. You paste credentials for a compatible provider such as Volcengine Ark, Alibaba Wan, SiliconFlow, Google Gemini, Tencent Hunyuan, Baidu Qianfan, OpenAI Images, xAI Images, fal.ai, Replicate, Stability AI, or a Custom JSON endpoint. Photos and prompts go from the device to that endpoint.
Data handling
There is no ImagineOutfit user account. API credentials stay in the iOS Keychain. Saved looks stay on the device. The developer does not receive photos or prompts in the normal generation flow. The chosen provider may associate requests with your provider account. See the Privacy Policy.
Languages
The in-app interface supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Arabic, and Hindi, or it can follow the iPhone language.
What it is not
ImagineOutfit is not a closet inventory, not a guaranteed virtual fitting room, not a social network, not a shopping app, and not a hosted credit-selling generation service. AI images are styling inspiration only.
